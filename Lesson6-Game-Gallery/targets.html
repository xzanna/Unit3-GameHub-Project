GameHub Day 6 targets: the game gallery

Each step before and after. Your pictures and colours can differ; the panel turns a row green when the rule is right.

Step 1: Make it a grid

display: grid and a gap on .gallery. Panel row 1.

Step 2: Columns that fit

repeat(auto-fit, minmax(240px, 1fr)). The grid counts the columns, not you. Panel row 2.

Step 3: The featured game

grid-column: span 2 on .tile-featured, the first tile only. Panel row 3.

Step 4: One shape for every picture

width 100%, a height, object-fit: cover. Panel row 4.

Step 5: Make each tile a card

A background, a border-radius, overflow: hidden and a shadow. Panel row 5.

Step 6: The caption

Padding inside the card, margin 0 on the heading, both lines readable (contrast 4.5+). Panel row 6.
